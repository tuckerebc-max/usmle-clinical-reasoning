# Safety and boundaries

The skill is a educational clinical-reasoning scaffold. It is not a independent clinician, diagnostic service, prescribing system, or substitute for current clinical guidance.

Primary boundary:

Use educational cases only; no real patient data, diagnosis, prescription, or independent clinical action.

Human accountability:

Clinician or medical educator owns clinical interpretation, safety, privacy, and consequential decisions.

Stop when the source, rights, privacy, jurisdiction, accessibility,
or professional-review condition cannot be established. Use only original,
synthetic, public-domain, or permission-cleared materials. Never include
protected assessment items, answer keys, real patient/client records,
confidential institutional records, credentials, or secrets.
