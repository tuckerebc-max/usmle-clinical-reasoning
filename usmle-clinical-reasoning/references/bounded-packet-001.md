## `OPT-66-USMLE` — Supervised clinical reasoning with missing cues

**Learner target:** Represent an educational case, state information needs, and keep safety and supervision visible.

**Fictional educational case:** A simulated learner receives a short case with a symptom pattern, two relevant findings, one missing time-course detail, and a prompt asking what information should be clarified before any next step.

**Learner task:** Build a cue and hypothesis ledger, identify the missing detail, explain why it matters, state what cannot be concluded, and draft a supervised handoff that requests qualified review.

**Required artifact:** case orientation, problem representation, hypothesis comparison, information-need rationale, safety / supervision record, reassessment question.

**Nonmodel:** A diagnosis or treatment instruction for a real person.  
**Feedback move:** Ask “What is known, what is inferred, what is missing, and who must review before action?”

**Agent handoff:** Agent may structure the fictional case; it must not diagnose, prescribe, or suppress uncertainty. Real-person input routes to professional review.

**Exit test:** Remove one cue and add a conflicting cue. Learner must preserve the conflict and revise the information plan.
